The product

Everything your outreach needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Designed around the part that actually matters: turning good prospects into thoughtful conversations without creating another spreadsheet monster.

01

Prospect intelligence

Keep contact details, notes, verified observations and conversation state together. No scattered tabs, no mystery context.

02

Draft → approve → send

Drafts stay drafts until a human approves them. Your voice stays yours and every send has a clear trail.

03

Follow-ups that behave

Queue the next step, pause it when someone replies and respect do-not-contact decisions automatically.

A calmer workflow

From first lead to real conversation.

Kyro keeps the workflow visible, deliberate and easy to hand off between people.

01

Add the prospect

Capture the person, company, channel and the one observation that makes the outreach worth sending.

02

Create the draft

Generate a grounded first pass from information you actually provided — not invented research.

03

Review the queue

See exactly what is waiting for approval, what is scheduled and what needs your attention.

04

Learn from replies

Replies and outcomes move the prospect forward and stop unnecessary follow-ups from piling up.

Built with restraint

Automation should give you leverage, not take the wheel.

Kyro is designed around explicit approval, scoped data, safe sending limits and a visible activity trail.
